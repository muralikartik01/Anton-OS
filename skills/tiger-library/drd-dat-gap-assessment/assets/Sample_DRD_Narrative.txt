Objective:
Establish a reusable reference dataset for product identifiers, product names, and core classification fields used across reporting workflows.

Scope:
This sample narrative is intentionally generic. It demonstrates how a DRD narrative can describe the business purpose, scope boundaries, and attribute expectations without assuming a specific project, market, or product family.

Business Process:
The source system publishes product master records. The target workflow standardizes core attributes, validates identifiers, and exposes a curated reference table for downstream reporting and planning use cases.
